Abstract

Strong gravitational lensing reveals important information about the universe, but reconstructing the source image and lens properties requires solving an ill-posed inverse imaging problem. Generative image models serve as powerful data-driven priors in Bayesian inverse problems. Diffusion models have been used widely, but they are computationally costly. Flow matching has emerged as a promising alternative to diffusion models, with easier training, efficient sampling, and better-quality data generation. In this work, we present Blind-FlowSGS, a principled blind posterior sampling method using flow matching priors, enabling efficient posterior sampling for non-blind and blind inversion of strong gravitational lenses. On simulated Euclid data, we demonstrate our method's improvement over diffusion-based methods in both efficacy and efficiency. Our method shows promising capabilities to accurately process strong lensing data from modern sky surveys at scale.
